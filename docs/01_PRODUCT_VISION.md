# Infinity AI Cloud Academy

# Product Vision

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Last Updated: July 2026

---

# 1. Vision

To become India's leading AI-powered EdTech platform that enables students and working professionals to master Data Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, and modern software technologies through practical learning, real-world projects, and industry mentorship.

---

# 2. Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between traditional education and industry requirements by providing:

- Practical Learning
- Real Projects
- Career Guidance
- AI Assisted Learning
- Placement Support
- Lifetime Learning Resources

---

# 3. Core Values

## Practical First

Students should build projects instead of only watching videos.

---

## Industry Focused

Every course should solve real business problems.

---

## Continuous Learning

Content should continuously evolve with technology.

---

## Community Driven

Students should collaborate through projects, discussions, and mentorship.

---

## AI Assisted

Every student should have access to an AI Mentor.
